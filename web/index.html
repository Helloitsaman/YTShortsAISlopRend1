04 · Scene notes

Script beats and angle notes

Scene notes will appear when an idea is ready.
These notes help shape the script. The still images themselves are chosen after you approve the script: the agent generates ElevenLabs narration, divides it into 10–15 timed beats, and finds one licensed image per beat, which you review in the next step before any video is built.
05 · Approve script & find stills

Generate narration, then match licensed stills to the speech

This generates the narration and its word timing, divides it into beats, and finds a licensed still for each. Nothing is rendered or uploaded yet — you review the stills first.
06 · Review stills

Review the still for each narration beat

Each image appears only while its phrase is spoken. Replace a still with your own licensed image, retry the search for a single beat, or remove a beat. Every beat needs an image before you can build. Source and license are shown under each still; provenance is saved in media_manifest.json. Nothing here guarantees the video is copyright-safe.
07 · Preview & upload

Inspect the finished short, then upload

The preview appears here after you approve the stills. The video is not uploaded until you review the preview and press Upload.
